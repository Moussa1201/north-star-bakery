OUR PEOPLE AND OUR PROCESS

A Bakery for Our Neighbors

Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: a neighborhood deserves a place to pick up good bread and feel welcome. We bake in small batches for busy mornings, family meals, and the celebrations that bring people together.

How we choose our ingredients

We look for dependable flour, good butter, and seasonal fruit. We buy from nearby suppliers when practical and choose ingredients for their flavor and freshness.

Have an ingredient question? Ask us before placing an order so we can help you understand what goes into each item.
